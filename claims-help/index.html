After an accident or loss

Five steps to take

1. Get safe

Check that everyone is okay. Call 911 if anyone is hurt or the scene isn't safe.

2. Document it

Take photos, and exchange names, phone numbers, insurance and vehicle details with anyone involved.

3. Prevent more damage

For a home loss, stop the water or cover the opening if it's safe, and keep your receipts.

4. Report the claim

Call the claims number on your insurance card or use your company's app. Most are open 24/7.

5. Let us know

Call (240) 842-9293 so we can help you through the claim and answer questions along the way.

Questions

Good to know

What should I do right after a car accident?

Make sure everyone is safe and call 911 if anyone is hurt. Exchange names, phone numbers, insurance and vehicle details with the other driver, take photos, then report the claim to your insurance company using the number on your insurance card or its app. Let us know too, so we can help.

Who do I call to file a claim?

Your insurance company's claims line is the fastest way to open a claim, and most are open 24/7. The number is on your insurance card or in the company's app. Tram's can help you through the process and answer questions.

Can I reach Tram's after hours?

Yes. When the office is closed or busy, our virtual assistant Vera answers (240) 842-9293 in English or French, takes your details and gets your message to the team, who follow up the next business day.

How do I get a new ID card or make a policy change?

Call or message the office. If we're busy or closed, Vera takes your request and the team follows up. Never send Social Security, license or payment numbers by email or text.
