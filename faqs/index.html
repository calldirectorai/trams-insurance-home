How do I update or change my policy?

Call the office or send us a message. If we're busy or closed, Vera takes your request and the team follows up.

What should I do after an accident?

Make sure everyone is safe and call 911 if anyone is hurt. Then call the claims number on your insurance card or app, which works 24/7, and let us know so we can help.

What's the difference between a surety bond and insurance?

Insurance protects you. A surety bond protects the party you're working for, by guaranteeing you'll meet an obligation. Bonds involve three parties: the principal (you), the obligee (who requires the bond) and the surety (who backs it).
