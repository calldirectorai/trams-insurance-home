Germantown, Maryland

Your local agent for Germantown

Germantown is one of Montgomery County's largest communities, and much of it is townhomes and condos inside homeowners associations, with a lot of residents commuting on I-270 or the MARC Brunswick Line every day.

That mix shapes what people need from an insurance agent: auto rates that reflect a long daily commute, condo and HO-6 policies that line up with an association's master policy, and renters coverage that apartment communities ask for at move-in. Because Germantown is unincorporated, rental homes here are licensed through Montgomery County's housing department, and landlords need a proper landlord policy to go with it.
