Damascus sits in upcounty Montgomery County along MD-27 and MD-124, close to the county's Agricultural Reserve. Homes here are often on larger lots, many are older, and many rely on private wells and septic systems rather than public utilities.

Those details change what a good policy looks like: rebuild costs set correctly for a larger or custom home, coverage for detached garages, barns and outbuildings, and protection for well pumps and equipment. Questions

Good to know

Does homeowners insurance cover my barn or detached garage in Damascus?

Most policies include some coverage for other structures, usually a percentage of the home's coverage. Larger barns, workshops or outbuildings may need higher limits, and farm operations may need a separate policy.

Is hitting a deer covered by car insurance?

Yes, under comprehensive coverage, not collision. If you only carry liability, a deer collision would not be covered.
