Your local agent for Montgomery Village

Montgomery Village is a planned community next to Gaithersburg, with neighborhoods of single-family homes, townhomes and condominiums around Lake Whetstone and the village's parks and pools. Common areas are managed by the Montgomery Village Foundation, and many homes are also part of a neighborhood association or condo regime.

That layered structure is why insurance here deserves a careful look. Your coverage should match what your association insures and what it expects you to insure, including your share of a master policy deductible if damage starts in your unit. We review the documents with you and set limits that close the gaps.

Questions

Good to know

Does my Montgomery Village townhome need a condo policy or a homeowners policy?

It depends on how your association is set up. Some townhomes are condominiums and need an HO-6, while others are fee-simple and need a homeowners policy. Send us your association documents and we will confirm.

Can you help with an association's insurance requirements?

Yes. We read the insurance section of your bylaws or declaration and make sure your policy matches what the association requires owners to carry.
