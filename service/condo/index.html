What condo coverage can include

Interior & improvements

Walls, flooring, cabinets, fixtures and upgrades not covered by the association.

Personal belongings

Furniture, electronics, clothing and valuables, against theft, fire and other covered losses.

Liability

Covers you if someone is hurt in your unit or you accidentally damage another unit.

Loss of use

Helps pay for temporary housing if your condo becomes unlivable after a covered loss.

Loss assessment

Helps pay your share if the association charges owners for a covered loss.

Valuables

Extra protection for jewelry, collectibles and high-value items.

Why condo insurance matters

Without it, you could pay out of pocket for repairs, replacements or legal costs inside your unit, even when the building itself is insured.

Maryland & Montgomery County

Condo insurance and Maryland's $5,000 deductible rule

Condo and townhome communities are everywhere in Gaithersburg, Germantown and Montgomery Village, and each association's master policy is different. Some cover only the walls, others include fixtures and finishes.

Maryland law adds a detail most owners miss: when damage starts in your unit, the association can charge you its master policy deductible, up to $5,000. We read your association's documents and set your HO-6 coverage to close that gap.

Loss assessment

Questions

Good to know

Isn't my condo covered by the association?

Usually only the building and common areas. Your interior, belongings and liability need your own policy.

Does my lender require condo insurance?

Many lenders do. We can send proof of insurance directly to them.

Can my condo association charge me its deductible in Maryland?

Yes. If the damage originates in your unit, Maryland law lets the council of unit owners charge you its property deductible, up to $5,000. Loss assessment and deductible coverage on your HO-6 can pay that bill.
