What workers' comp covers

Medical expenses

Hospital bills, doctor visits, medication and rehab for work injuries.

Lost wages

Partial wage replacement while an employee recovers.

Disability benefits

Short-term or permanent disability from a workplace injury.

Ongoing care

Physical therapy and long-term treatment for serious injuries.

Death benefits

Financial support for families after a fatal workplace injury.

Employer liability

Legal defense and settlements if an employee sues over a work injury.

Maryland & Montgomery County

Workers' comp rules in Maryland

Maryland requires every employer with one or more employees to carry workers' compensation, with very few exceptions. Part-time employees count. Coverage must come from a company licensed in Maryland or an approved self-insurance plan.

The penalty for going without can be a fine of up to $10,000, and corporate officers can be held personally liable. We help new businesses get covered quickly and make sure your class codes and payroll are set correctly, which is where most overcharges come from.

Good to know

Is workers' comp required in Maryland?

Most Maryland employers with one or more employees must carry it. We can check what applies to your business.

How is the price set?

Mainly by your payroll and the type of work your employees do.

Do I need workers' comp for one part-time employee in Maryland?

Yes. Maryland requires coverage for employers with one or more employees, and part-time workers count.
