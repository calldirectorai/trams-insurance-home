Maryland & Montgomery County

RVs, boats and motorcycles in Maryland

A motorhome or motorcycle registered in Maryland must carry the same minimum liability as a car. Travel trailers and boats are different, and they are often left uninsured for physical damage until something happens.

Many local owners store RVs and boats off-site over the winter. We can adjust coverage for storage season and set full coverage again before your first trip to the Eastern Shore or Deep Creek.

Questions

Good to know

Does my auto insurance cover my RV or motorcycle?

Not fully. RVs and motorcycles need separate policies for full protection.

Can I pause coverage when my vehicle is stored?

Many companies offer lower-cost storage or lay-up coverage during the off-season. Ask us.

Does my motorcycle need insurance all year in Maryland?

A registered motorcycle must stay insured, but some companies offer lower lay-up rates while it is stored. If you turn in the tags instead, tell us first so you avoid an MVA lapse penalty.
