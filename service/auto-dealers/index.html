Maryland & Montgomery County

Maryland dealer bonds and dealer insurance

The Maryland MVA requires a dealer bond before it issues a dealer license. The amount depends on your license type and how many vehicles you sold last year: used dealers start at $15,000 for up to 250 sales, and new-vehicle dealers start at $50,000.

Beyond the bond, dealers need garage liability, dealer's open lot coverage for inventory, and dealer plates coverage for test drives. We put the pieces together so your license application and your coverage line up.

Used dealer bond

$15,000 (1-250 sales), $25,000 (251-500), $35,000 (501-1,000), $50,000 (1,001-2,500), $150,000 (2,501+).

New dealer bond

$50,000 (1-500 sales), $75,000 (501-1,000), $100,000 (1,001-2,500), $300,000 (2,501+).

Questions

Good to know

What's the difference between garage liability and garagekeepers?

Garage liability covers injuries and damage your operations cause to others. Garagekeepers covers customers' vehicles while they're in your care.

How much is a Maryland used car dealer bond?

The bond amount starts at $15,000 for up to 250 sales a year and rises with volume. Your premium is a small percentage of that amount, based mainly on credit.
