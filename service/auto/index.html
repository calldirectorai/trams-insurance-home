Coverage

What auto coverage can include

Liability

Pays for injuries and property damage you cause to others, required in Maryland.

Collision

Repairs your car after an accident, no matter who was at fault.

Comprehensive

Covers theft, vandalism, hail, falling trees and other non-collision damage.

Uninsured / underinsured motorist

Protects you when the other driver has little or no insurance.

Medical payments

Helps with medical bills for you and your passengers after an accident.

Roadside & rental

Towing, jump-starts, lockouts, and a rental car while yours is repaired.

Questions

Good to know

What does auto coverage include?

Liability is required in Maryland. Most drivers add collision and comprehensive, and options like roadside assistance and rental reimbursement.

Can I bundle auto with home or renters insurance?

Yes. Bundling often lowers your total price. Ask us to quote both together.

How fast can I get covered?

In many cases the same day, once we have your driver and vehicle details.

Do you help with SR-22 or FR-19 filings?

Yes. We work with companies that write high-risk policies and can handle the filing for you.
