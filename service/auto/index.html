Maryland & Montgomery County

Car insurance rules in Maryland, explained

Maryland requires every registered vehicle to carry insurance at all times, not just when you drive it. The state minimums are $30,000 per person and $60,000 per accident for bodily injury, $15,000 for property damage, matching uninsured motorist coverage, and $2,500 in Personal Injury Protection (PIP).

Those minimums are low for Montgomery County. A fender-bender with a newer SUV on I-270 or MD-355 can pass $15,000 in property damage on its own, so most of our clients choose higher limits. We show you the price difference side by side so you can decide.

Lapse penalties

If your coverage lapses, the MVA charges $150 for the first 30 days and $7 for each day after, up to $2,500, and can suspend your tags.

FR-19 requests

When the MVA sends an FR-19 asking for proof of insurance, we can respond for you with the right documents.

PIP choices

Maryland lets you waive full PIP for yourself in writing. We explain what you give up before you decide.

Questions

Good to know

What does auto coverage include?

Liability is required in Maryland. Most drivers add collision and comprehensive, and options like roadside assistance and rental reimbursement.

Can I bundle auto with home or renters insurance?

Yes. Bundling often lowers your total price. Ask us to quote both together.

How fast can I get covered?

In many cases the same day, once we have your driver and vehicle details.

Do you help with SR-22 or FR-19 filings?

Yes. We work with companies that write high-risk policies and can handle the filing for you.

What is the minimum car insurance in Maryland?

$30,000 per person and $60,000 per accident for bodily injury, $15,000 for property damage, uninsured motorist coverage at the same limits, and $2,500 of PIP.

What happens if my Maryland car insurance lapses?

The MVA charges $150 for the first 30 days and $7 per day after that, up to $2,500, and may suspend your registration. Call us before a lapse and we will help you keep coverage in force.
