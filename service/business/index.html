Questions

Good to know

What insurance does a small business need?

Most need general liability. Many also need workers' comp, commercial auto or property, depending on employees, vehicles and location.

What is a BOP?

A business owners policy bundles general liability and property coverage, often at a better price than buying them separately.

Do you provide certificates of insurance?

Yes. We issue certificates for clients, landlords and contracts.

What insurance does a small business need in Maryland?

Most need general liability and property coverage, often bundled as a BOP. Workers' comp is required once you have one employee, and commercial auto is needed for business-owned vehicles.
