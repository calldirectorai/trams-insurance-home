Maryland & Montgomery County

Why Montgomery County families add an umbrella

Maryland's minimum auto liability of $30,000 per person was set long ago. A serious accident or a guest injured at your home can produce a claim far larger than your auto or home limits, especially in a high-cost area like Montgomery County.

An umbrella adds $1 million or more on top of both policies for a modest annual cost. It is most valuable if you have a teen driver, a pool, a rental property, or savings you want to protect.

Sits on top

Broader coverage

Good to know

How much umbrella coverage do I need?

Many people start with $1 million. We'll look at your assets and risks and suggest an amount.

Do I need other policies first?

Yes. Umbrella coverage sits on top of your home and auto policies, which usually need certain liability limits.

How much umbrella coverage do I need?

Most families start with $1 million. If your home equity, savings and future earnings are higher than that, we look at $2 million or more.
