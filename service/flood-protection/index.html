Maryland & Montgomery County

Flood insurance in Montgomery County

You do not need to live on the coast to flood. Flash flooding along Great Seneca Creek, Muddy Branch, Rock Creek and their tributaries has damaged homes and cars across the county, and many flood claims nationally come from outside high-risk zones.

Standard homeowners and renters policies do not cover flood. We quote National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policies and private flood options, and help you read your FEMA flood zone.

30-day wait

NFIP policies usually take 30 days to start, so buy before a storm is in the forecast.

Renters too

Contents-only flood coverage is available for renters and condo owners.

Lender requirement

If you are in a high-risk zone with a federally backed mortgage, your lender will require it.

Questions

Good to know

Does homeowners insurance cover flooding?

No. Flood damage needs a separate flood policy.

Do I need flood insurance outside a flood zone?

It's worth considering. Floods happen outside high-risk zones too, and coverage there is often less expensive.

Is there a waiting period for flood insurance?

Yes. A new NFIP policy typically takes 30 days to take effect, with limited exceptions such as a policy bought at a home purchase. Private flood policies may have shorter waits.
