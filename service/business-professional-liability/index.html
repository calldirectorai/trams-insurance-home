What liability coverage can include

General liability

Bodily injury and property damage claims from your work or premises.

Professional liability (E&O)

Legal defense

Maryland & Montgomery County

Liability coverage for Maryland professionals and contractors

General liability covers bodily injury and property damage. Professional liability (errors and omissions) covers financial harm from your advice or work: a missed deadline, a design mistake, a bookkeeping error. Many Montgomery County clients and government contracts require both.

We work with consultants, IT firms, tax preparers, real estate professionals, contractors and healthcare practices, and match your limits to what your contracts actually require.
