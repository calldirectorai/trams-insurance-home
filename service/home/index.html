What types of damage are not covered under standard homeowners insurance?

Damage from floods, earthquakes, and neglect (like poor maintenance) is not covered. These require separate policies or preventative care.

Is liability protection included in homeowners insurance?

Yes. Liability coverage helps pay for injuries or property damage that you or your family members (including pets) may cause to others.

How do I determine the right coverage amount for my home?

Our agents will work with you to assess the cost of rebuilding your home and replacing your belongings, so you get just the right level of protection.
