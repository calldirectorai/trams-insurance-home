Homeowners insurance in Montgomery County

From 1960s ranches in Gaithersburg to newer townhomes in Clarksburg, homes here face the same handful of risks: heavy summer storms, falling trees, ice dams, and water backing up into finished basements.

A standard homeowners policy covers wind, fire, theft and liability, but it leaves gaps that matter in this area. We check rebuild cost against today's construction prices, then look at the add-ons that local homeowners use most.

Water backup

Sewer and sump-pump backup is excluded from most base policies. It is a low-cost add-on and one we recommend for finished basements.

Flood is separate

Homeowners policies do not cover flood. Homes near Great Seneca Creek, Muddy Branch or Rock Creek should price a flood policy too.

Questions

Good to know

Does homeowners insurance cover floods?

No. Standard policies exclude flood damage. We can add a separate flood policy.

How much coverage do I need?

Enough to rebuild your home at today's costs, which can be different from its market value. We help you estimate it.

Can I save by bundling?

Usually, yes. Quoting home and auto together often lowers your total price.

Does my Maryland homeowners policy cover a flooded basement?

Not from flooding, and usually not from sewer backup unless you add that endorsement. We can quote both a water backup endorsement and a separate flood policy.
