Event insurance for Montgomery County venues

Most venues in Montgomery County ask for a certificate of insurance before they confirm your date. Montgomery Parks, for example, requires a certificate naming M-NCPPC as additional insured for every event held on parkland.

We write one-day and multi-day liability for weddings, birthdays, festivals, vendor booths and community events, and send the certificate straight to the venue.
