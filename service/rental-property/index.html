What landlord coverage can include

Dwelling

Protects the physical structure of your rental property.

Landlord liability

Covers you if a tenant or guest is injured and holds you responsible.

Loss of rental income

Replaces rent if the property becomes unlivable after a covered loss.

Weather, fire & vandalism

Protection from storm, fire, water and vandalism damage.

Optional add-ons

Appliance coverage, legal fee protection and more.

Renters insurance

For tenants: belongings, liability and temporary housing, at a low cost.

Is landlord insurance required?

If there's a mortgage on the property, your lender will likely require it. Even without one, it protects your investment and your income.

Good to know

Does my homeowners policy cover a property I rent out?

Usually not. A rented property needs a landlord policy.

Should my tenants have renters insurance?

It's a good idea. Their belongings aren't covered by your landlord policy.
