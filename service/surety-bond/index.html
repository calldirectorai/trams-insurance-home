Surety bonds

What is a surety bond?

A surety bond is a guarantee that an obligation will be met. It involves three parties: the principal (you), the obligee (the party requiring the bond) and the surety (the company that backs it).

Bonds are often required to get licensed, bid on government work or sign a construction contract. We help you get the right bond, quickly.

Maryland & Montgomery County

Surety bonds for Maryland licenses and contracts

Many Maryland licenses require a bond before the state will issue them, including MVA dealer and title service licenses. Contractors bidding on county, state or federal work often need bid, performance and payment bonds as well.

A bond is a guarantee to the state or project owner, not insurance for you. Premiums are usually a small percentage of the bond amount, and most license bonds can be issued quickly once we have your application.

Questions

Good to know

How is a bond different from insurance?

Insurance protects you. A bond protects the party you're working for, by guaranteeing you'll meet your obligation.

How long does a bond last?

It depends on the bond. Many stay active until the obligation is met or the term ends.

How much does a surety bond cost?

Most license bonds cost a small percentage of the bond amount per year, based mainly on your credit. A $15,000 dealer bond, for example, often costs a few hundred dollars.
